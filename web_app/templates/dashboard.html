Welcome to ChartHawk

Please read and accept these terms before continuing.

ChartHawk lets you upload any chart image and get an AI-powered analysis that detects misleading design choices, flags truncated axes, cherry-picked date ranges and distorted scales, and tells you what the data actually shows.

What happens to your uploaded images
Images you upload are sent to an AI model provider for analysis. They are also stored in your ChartHawk account so you can revisit previous analyses. By using ChartHawk you agree to Anthropic's usage policies.

Data storage
Your charts and conversation history are stored in a database linked to your account. Only you can access your data unless you make an analysis public. We use Supabase for data storage.

Deleting your data
You can delete individual charts at any time. To delete your account and all data, send us feedback requesting account deletion. We will remove your data within 30 days.

No warranties
ChartHawk is provided as-is. AI analyses may contain errors. Always apply your own judgment.
